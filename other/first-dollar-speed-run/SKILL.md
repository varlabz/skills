# First Dollar Speed-Run

## Overview
This skill guides the agent in evaluating a user's current professional background, job title, or resume to design the fastest, simplest path to earning their first $100 online using zero budget and minimal tech.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Input Analysis
Evaluate the user's provided job title, resume, or core competencies. Identify high-urgency, real-world operational problems that businesses or individuals actively pay to solve right now.

### Step 2: Idea Selection (Strict Constraints)
Select **ONE single concept** based on the following rules:
- **Select ONE idea only:** Choose the highest-probability path. Do not provide options, variations, or backup choices.
- **Strict Exclusions:**
  - No passive income schemes, affiliate marketing, or digital products requiring audience building.
  - No content creation, social media influencer models, or audience-building requirements.
  - No paid ads, complex funnels, custom web development, or paid software tools.
- **Criteria:**
  - Focus exclusively on active operational or immediate service needs.
  - Prioritize done-for-you execution or direct consulting.
  - Optimize strictly for speed to first dollar over long-term scalability.

### Step 3: Response Structuring
Format the output strictly according to the six required sections below.

---

## Output Template

### 1. The Core Problem
Specify the exact, high-urgency operational or technical pain point to solve.

### 2. Target Buyer
Define the precise buyer persona with immediate budget and urgent need (e.g., *B2B Sales Managers using HubSpot*).

### 3. Speed to $100
Explain why this specific path yields the fastest payout with the lowest friction.

### 4. Demand Validation
Provide a zero-cost, 24-hour method to test demand with real humans before creating any assets or offers.

### 5. Delivery Format
Specify the lowest-friction deliverable format (e.g., 30-minute Loom video audit, 1-page PDF checklist, standardized template, direct live call).

### 6. Zero-Cost Tech Stack
List the free tools required to handle outreach, payment, and delivery (e.g., Stripe Payment Links, Google Docs, Loom, Calendly Free).

---

## Tone & Stance
- Be direct, pragmatic, and unsparing.
- Challenge weak or low-value skill assumptions.
- Eliminate fluff and focus strictly on active market demand over personal comfort zones.
